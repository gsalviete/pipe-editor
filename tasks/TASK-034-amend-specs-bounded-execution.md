# [DOC] Amend specs for bounded, recoverable execution (Phase 5)

## What this card solves

Phase 5 (AR-25, 26, 27) adds limits and lifecycle guarantees that the specs don't state:
- system-wide caps on runs, output and subscribers;
- a slow-client policy for SSE;
- copy budgets;
- abort that waits for confirmed container removal;
- Docker readiness that proves the daemon (not just the CLI);
- stream recovery without duplicated output.

The review stresses that stage timeouts and resource isolation are **v1 non-goals**, and the product doc keeps them out. This card must write limits for the registry, streams and copies **without** smuggling in timeouts or CPU/memory caps. It also settles the product doc's open question on the concrete budget values.
